Rules

Respect The City. Respect The Story.

Bloodline RP is serious roleplay. Stay in character, play fair, and build meaningful interactions that push story forward.

Apply Now Visit Store

Core Rule

No RDM / VDM

Random violence without story context is prohibited. Every major action must have roleplay weight.

Core Rule

No Powergaming

Do not force outcomes or perform actions your character could not realistically do.

Core Rule

Value Of Life

Act like your character values survival. Reckless behavior without reason is fail roleplay.

Core Rule

No Metagaming

Use only information your character learns in roleplay. Outside info must stay outside.

Enforcement

Fair But Strict Moderation

Staff reviews incidents with logs and clips. Repeated violations escalate from warnings to suspensions or permanent bans.
