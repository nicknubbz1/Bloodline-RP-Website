Server Standards

Serious scenes require serious rules.

These standards protect consistency and trust. Follow them to keep conflict fair, roleplay believable, and outcomes worth investing in.

Apply with confidence Open account hub

Enforcement

Evidence Driven

Moderation actions use logs, clips, and context-based review.

Progressive Action

Discipline scales from warnings to bans based on severity and repetition.

Core conduct rules

Know these before joining high-stakes scenes.

No RDM / VDM

Violence always needs believable in-character reasoning and escalation.

No Metagaming

Use only knowledge your character could realistically obtain.

No Powergaming

Do not force outcomes or ignore realistic physical limits.

Value of Life

Characters must treat danger and injury as meaningful and serious.

Respect Players

Compete in character while keeping out-of-character behavior mature.

Follow Staff

When moderators step in, cooperate quickly and respectfully.

What strong RP actually looks like

Strong roleplay is not about always winning scenes. It is about decisions, consequences, and collaborative pressure that other characters can respond to naturally.
