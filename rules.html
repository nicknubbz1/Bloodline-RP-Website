Rulebook

Respect The Story, Respect The City

These standards keep Bloodline organized and fair. They protect scene quality, player trust, and long-term progression for everyone.

Apply After Reading Link Account

Quick Standard

Minimum Expectation

Stay in character Avoid low-effort conflict Use realistic decision-making Respect outcome fairness

Core Rule

No RDM / VDM

Violence without roleplay context is prohibited. Force must always be tied to a believable storyline.

Core Rule

No Powergaming

Do not force outcomes or perform unrealistic actions your character could not actually execute.

Core Rule

Value Of Life

Characters should preserve their life and avoid reckless behavior without valid in-character reasons.

Core Rule

No Metagaming

Out-of-character information cannot be used in roleplay scenes, investigations, or planning.

Enforcement

Fair And Evidence-Based

Moderation actions use logs, clips, and context. Repeat violations escalate from warnings to suspensions and bans.

  • Step 1: Warning and guidance.
  • Step 2: Temporary suspension.
  • Step 3: Long suspension or permanent removal.

Scene Quality

How To Keep RP Strong

  • Give other players room to respond in scenes.
  • Use escalation before force when possible.
  • Keep communication clear and in-character.
  • Report issues through proper channels, not scene disruption.
