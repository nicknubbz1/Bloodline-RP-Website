Login With Steam. Then Connect Discord.

Your Bloodline account name is your Steam name. After Steam login, the next step is linking Discord so whitelist access, applications, and community permissions stay synced.

Open Login See Account Status

Step 1

Steam Login

Launch real Steam authentication through the Bloodline auth server. Once Steam returns successfully, your website account name will use your Steam display name.

If you are already signed into Steam, the popup should only need one confirm click.

Step 2

Connect Discord

After Steam succeeds, launch Discord OAuth to attach the community account used for whitelist roles, support, and application tracking.

Discord linking stays locked until Steam authentication has completed.
