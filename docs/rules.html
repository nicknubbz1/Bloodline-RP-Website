Combat Logging

If your character goes down you must stay in the server, receive medical help, and complete the scene.

You cannot leave the city to save your inventory or respawn to avoid further interactions. This includes disconnecting to avoid PD custody, being robbed by rivals, and similar situations.

Do not participate in a scene if you do not have adequate time to complete it.

Characters involved in criminal activity must be completely clear of any law enforcement before disconnecting.

Accidental disconnects, connection interruptions, or crashes should be communicated via a Discord ticket immediately.

Fail Roleplay

Do not do anything that would be considered unrealistic. Bloodline RP is a serious RP server and we expect you to treat it as such.

Examples

  • Failing to respond to /me actions.
  • Lying on /me actions for your advantage.
  • Spamming /try for desired results.
  • Opening car doors while cuffed.
  • Talking OOC in city scenes.
  • Denying RP responses.

Breaking Character

Breaking character in any way is not acceptable.

If you run into an issue, play out the scene and once the scene is fully finished open a staff ticket in help-and-support to report concerns.

Remember you are playing a character, not yourself. Any talk about IRL life in city is prohibited.

Take OOC matters to Discord DMs or tickets only when necessary.

Always use in-city terms. Do not say things such as "that NPC was not there on my screen."

Random Death Match (RDM)

  • There will be no killing or attacking anyone without roleplay leading up to it.
  • There must be valid RP reasoning and a previous altercation to shoot and kill someone.

Vehicle Death Match (VDM)

  • VDM is intentionally running someone over with a vehicle with intent to kill.
  • Under no circumstances is VDM allowed in this server.
  • Failure to follow this rule may result in disciplinary action.

Poor Roleplay

Always remember the point of RP is to have fun. Enter scenes with intent to make them enjoyable for all parties. Going in with a win-only mindset is poor RP.

Examples

  • Fake hostage scenarios.
  • Not roleplaying your injuries.
  • Camping locations.
  • Pocket wiping.
  • Doing crime on bikes as an easy getaway.
  • Prioritizing gun-play over roleplay.

New Life Rule

Upon respawning (air lifting to the hospital), you forget everything that led up to being downed.

Your character has no knowledge of what happened leading up to being downed, therefore you may not retaliate or return to the scene.

You may not report that incident to police as your character remembers nothing.

If you are treated by medical and remain in scene, you may remember the events.

Power Gaming

This is when you prioritize your own progress in a way that excludes or limits others from proper roleplay. It also includes using game mechanics to gain unfair advantage.

Examples

  • Using /try incorrectly for personal gain.
  • Using /me incorrectly for personal gain.
  • Forcing a player to transfer money from bank or person.
  • Altering a scenario to remove another character's story involvement.
  • Abusing in-game mechanics for advantage.

Metagaming

This is when outside information is brought in city to gain an advantage.

Examples

  • Using Discord calls for in-city information.
  • Receiving or sending DMs with in-city advantage details.
  • Watching somebody stream and using that knowledge in city.

Banned Scenarios

Zero-tolerance banned scenarios include the following and will result in immediate disciplinary action.

  • Suicide roleplay.
  • Sexual assault roleplay.
  • Discrimination based on sex, gender, race, disabilities, slurs, and similar hate behavior.

Denying Roleplay

While you are in server, you are expected to bring quality roleplay to the table even if upset.

You must roleplay scenarios fully from start to finish. You may not act in ways that prevent the other party from roleplaying with you.

Examples

  • Getting downed by AI and telling EMS to just pick you up without RP.
  • Killing someone and dumping the body in the ocean without proper RP.
  • Mic spamming to stop interaction from occurring.

Force Roleplay

This is engaging in RP that the other person does not condone.

Car jacking and robberies do not require consent.

Examples

  • Forcing outcomes that drastically alter another character's storyline.
  • Forcing non-repairable injuries.

If you want to create a storyline with drastic alteration, communicate OOC through Discord first and get approval.

Cop Baiting

Doing actions with sole intent of grabbing cop attention is not allowed.

Examples

  • Intentionally speeding past officers.
  • Driving by traffic stops to antagonize officers.
  • Following or interfering in cop chases unrelated to you.

Fear For Life / No Value Of Life

In deadly scenarios, proceed with caution and realistic fear for life.

If you can reasonably make a quick escape, you may attempt to leave. If not, comply reasonably with aggressors.

The lower advantage side should concede and comply when appropriate.

Just because you know OOC your character can survive does not mean your character knows that in scene.

Abuse Of Server Scripts

Using scripts against intended design is prohibited. Any misuse or abuse may result in disciplinary action.

Examples

  • Using emotes to pass through locked doors, fences, or solid structures.
  • Parking a vehicle in garage to fix body damage.
  • Passing items through walls or windows.
  • Using emotes such as sleep/prone for gunfight advantage.

Cheating / Exploiting

Cheating or exploiting of any kind is never allowed.

Examples

  • Mod menus.
  • Abusing bugs in the server.
  • Using crosshairs not provided by Bloodline in city.

Fun And Fairness

At all times, ensure your actions are fun for both sides. Bloodline is not about winning; it is about quality RP for everyone involved.

Examples

  • Robbing people for items/money without valid RP reasoning and prior story history.
  • Criminals taking one jump to lose cops 30 seconds into a chase.
  • Cops ramming a criminal vehicle 30 seconds into a chase with intent to disable.

PVP Rule

When engaging in PVP activity you may have no more than 6 people per side, 12 total.

The same 12 who start are the same 12 who finish. Once someone goes down, no one can replace that player mid-fight.

Gang RP

Each gang (official or unofficial) can have unlimited member slots.

If a gang HQ is under attack, the standard PVP limit is voided for defenders only.

  • Aggressors attacking another gang HQ are still limited to 6.
  • The defending gang may use as many available members as needed to defend territory.

If you are an unofficial gang and want official status with more perks, complete the gang application.
