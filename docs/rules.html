Community Standards

Consistency, realism, and respect first.

Rules are here to protect scene quality and player trust. These standards are enforced to keep roleplay fair, immersive, and sustainable for everyone.

Moderation Policy

Evidence based

Actions are judged using logs, context, and scene-level impact instead of assumptions.

Progressive enforcement

Warnings, suspensions, and bans scale with severity and repeat behavior.

Core roleplay rules

Know these before entering serious scenes.

No RDM or VDM

Violence always needs roleplay context and believable intent.

No Metagaming

Use only information your character could reasonably know in-world.

No Powergaming

Do not force impossible outcomes or ignore realistic constraints.

Value of Life

Treat danger and injury seriously. Your character should avoid reckless nonsense.

Respect All Players

Compete in character, but keep out-of-character behavior mature and respectful.

Comply with Staff

When staff intervene, cooperate quickly so issues can be resolved cleanly.

What great roleplay looks like

Tension with realism, conflict with intent, outcomes with consequence.

High-quality RP is not about winning scenes. It is about creating pressure, response, and character growth in ways that other players can build on.
