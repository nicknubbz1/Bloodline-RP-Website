Rules

Stay in character. Play fair. Build better stories.

Bloodline RP is a serious environment. The rules are here to keep the city consistent, fair, and fun for everyone involved.

Apply NowVisit Store

Enforcement

Fair but firm

Staff review incidents based on logs, context, and roleplay impact.

Escalation

Warnings, suspensions, and bans are all handled with consistency and care.

Core standards

These are the pillars of good roleplay.

No RDM / VDM

Violence needs context, intention, and a roleplay reason behind it.

No Powergaming

Do not force outcomes your character could not realistically achieve.

No Metagaming

Use only information your character could lawfully learn in the world.

Value of life

Act like survival matters. Reckless actions without reason hurt the scene.

Respect the space

Keep interactions fair and treat other players as part of the story, not obstacles.

Follow staff

When staff intervene, cooperate and allow the situation to be resolved cleanly.

Roleplay expectations

The city works best when everyone plays with intention.

Good roleplay is about momentum, consequence, and realism. The stronger the standards, the more believable the world becomes.
