Welcome To Bloodline RP

Serious Roleplay Built Around Story And Community

Bloodline RP was created after years of server hopping and never finding one place that got every major piece right. We built this server to combine strong systems, serious standards, and meaningful story progression in one city.

Whether you run civilian life, criminal arcs, or law enforcement scenes, the goal is the same: keep every path active, engaging, and worth investing in long-term.
